Each order moves through clear statuses, which you can follow on your orders page:

An order can contain items from more than one supplier. Each supplier fulfils its own lines, and the order shows as Shipped once every line is done. If every line is cancelled, the whole order shows as Cancelled.

You can cancel an order yourself while it's still Pending. After that, contact the HubWeld team. When the parcel arrives, mark the order received so it closes as Completed.

Tips for maintenance and plant buyers

If you buy welding supplies for a plant, a shop or a maintenance crew, a few habits save money and downtime:

  1. Standardize your consumables. Pick one wire diameter, one contact tip size and one nozzle per gun model. Fewer part numbers means fewer wrong orders. Our guide to sourcing welding consumables covers how to set this up.
  2. Order consumables and PPE together. Combining a month of tips, nozzles, wire, gloves and lenses gets most small shops past the CAD $500 free-shipping threshold.
  3. Keep a minimum stock level. Set a reorder point for each fast-moving item (for example, two spools of 0.035 in. ER70S-6 and 50 contact tips) and order when you hit it.
  4. Apply for Net 30 if you buy often. Choose "Request Net 30 invoice" at checkout. It's subject to account approval, and once approved it lets your accounts payable team pay on terms.
  5. Replace PPE on schedule. Auto-darkening helmets used in Canada should meet CSA Z94.3. Cover lenses, gloves and respirator filters wear out faster than people think. See our list of welding safety essentials.
  6. Use the notes field. PO numbers, receiving hours and tax exemptions belong in the order notes, not in a follow-up email that gets lost.
